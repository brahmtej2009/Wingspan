# Wingspan Development Notice

### Stardance Ends
---

Wingspan was a community loved project at Hack Club's stardance, which was an event in partnership with NASA, MIT, and other organizations. Thanks to stardance, Wingspan had a platform to achieve multiple milestones throughout its development journey. 


## Future Updates
Wingspan has been created to a level that it is fit for public use, and includes all features required for daily use. Now, Wingspan would be taking a developmental pause, as Hack Club's Stardance ended on 31st October 2026.

Nonetheless, Wingspan would be back in development to have amazing new features and updates as soon as a newer event rolls in, giving me a platform to show my work and get responses from the world!

I would like to thank Hack Club, The Voters, Shipwrights, GOIs, and the entire community for showing this much love to wingspan. Your appreciation drives these amazing projects forward!
