# Hello Voters!

This is Wingspan! An automation software for managing your physical infrastructure/homelab/datacenter running Pterodactyl.
Wingspan provides you ability to take bulk actions on your pterodactyl, enabling you to manage hundreds of servers in one go.
Wingspan runs as a terminal tool, and hence, works in both Windows and Linux, in both GUI and CLI versions.

Wingspan is documented in great detail at Github!
